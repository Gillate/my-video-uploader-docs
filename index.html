My Video Uploader

My Video Uploader is a personal desktop tool that lets you upload and schedule your own videos to your own TikTok account, using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Features

  • Bulk upload videos from a local folder — filenames become post titles
  • Schedule posts for future publication
  • Secure authorization via TikTok Login (OAuth 2.0) — your tokens stay on your own computer

How it works

Run the app locally, log in with your TikTok account, select a folder of videos, and the app uploads them directly to TikTok via the official API. No video data ever passes through any server other than TikTok's own.
